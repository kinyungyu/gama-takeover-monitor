💼 Business Snapshot

What Gamma does:
  • Pan-European B2B cloud communications platform — UCaaS, SIP trunking, cloud PBX, CCaaS, connectivity
  • Channel-led model: 3,000+ partners across UK & Europe
  • Owns 2,500km fibre network + switching centres (London, Manchester, Glasgow)
  • Microsoft Teams Operator Connect partner (one of largest globally)
  • 89% recurring revenue, 54% gross margins, 22% EBITDA margins
Geographic split:
  • UK Business: Channel-led UCaaS/SIP for SMEs (largest segment)
  • UK Enterprise: Direct sales to larger accounts + AI deployments
  • Germany: Placetel + STARFACE — approaching ¼ of group GP. Double-digit organic growth
  • Spain: Gamma Comunicaciones (SIP + cloud PBX)
  • Netherlands: 900+ channel partners (ex-Dean One/gnTel)
  • Croatia: Entered 2024 (Adriatic beachhead)
PE appeal: Predictable recurring revenue, high cash conversion, low capex, fragmented European market for bolt-on M&A, PSTN switch-off tailwind, channel model = low customer acquisition cost. O2 Daisy deal (Sep 2025) repositioned as pure B2B wholesale/enterprise platform — cleaner for PE.
